## Context

RESIST is a streetwear brand from Bacolod that sells through its website, Facebook and Instagram messages, and in person at pop-ups. Filipino shoppers expect to pay with GCash, Maya or QR Ph, not just cards.

## Problem

Small drops sell out fast, so stock had to stay correct under pressure: no overselling when two people check out the last unit, no stock stuck in abandoned checkouts, and no orders lost to a late or repeated payment notification. And because many sales happen outside the website, the stock count had to stay true across every channel, not just online.

## What I did

- Built the storefront, checkout and admin in Next.js 16 with PostgreSQL and Drizzle ORM.
- Integrated PayMongo Checkout for GCash, Maya, QR Ph, card and GrabPay, with signed, idempotent webhooks; the success page also confirms payment directly so orders don't wait on a delayed webhook.
- Made stock holds **derived, not stored**: a checkout holds its units until it expires, and an abandoned checkout frees them the moment it does, with no cron job or queue.
- Locked the affected rows during checkout and payment (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`, in a fixed order) so concurrent shoppers can't both buy the last unit, and covered it with a concurrency test against a real database.
- Handled the edge case honestly: a payment that lands after its hold expired, when the stock already sold elsewhere, is still recorded as paid and flagged **Oversold** for the admin instead of being dropped.
- Built a "Record a sale" flow so in-person, Facebook, Instagram and pop-up sales come off the same shelf as website orders, in the same transaction, with the channel and the admin who entered it.
- Wrote every stock change to an auditable ledger, and added order confirmation emails through Gmail or Resend, a flat shipping fee, and admin-managed banner, gallery and testimonials.

## Impact

- One stock count is the source of truth for every channel the brand sells through.
- Overselling is prevented under concurrent checkouts, and abandoned checkouts never lock up stock.
- The store is live at [resist-store.vercel.app](https://resist-store.vercel.app).
